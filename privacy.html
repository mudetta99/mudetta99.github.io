Privacy Policy - Excel Backup

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Excel Backup is a local backup tool for construction-finishing accounts data, made for a single designated user.

What it accesses

The app requests full access to the Google Drive account you sign in with (view, edit, create and delete files). It uses this access to upload new backup copies of your records to your Drive. It does not delete your existing backups.

Where data is stored

Backup copies are stored in your own Google Drive account, which is operated by Google. A sign-in token is kept on the computer running the app so backups can continue.

Sharing

The app's only data transfer is uploading your backup copies to the Google Drive account you connect, for the backup purpose described above.
