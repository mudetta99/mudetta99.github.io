Excel Backup

Excel Backup is a small local Windows application for recording construction-finishing accounts (budgets, expenses and notes). It keeps backup copies of your records in the Google Drive account you sign in with.

You sign in through Google's own page. The app stores a sign-in token on your computer so it can keep uploading new backups. It does not delete your existing backups.
